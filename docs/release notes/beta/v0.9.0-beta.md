# Release Notes v0.9.0-beta

*April 13, 2026*

This first beta release includes basic account and tree functionality and includes localization for Dutch and English.

## Accounts

- Users can register for an account.
    - Passwords must adhere to a minimum safety standard.
- Users can log in to their accounts using their e-mail and password.
- Users can view a list of their own trees on the /my-trees page.
- Users can view their profile and edit their data on the /profile page.

## Trees

- Registered users can create and publish trees.
- Tree owners can edit their trees on their respective tree editor pages.
    - They can edit the metadata of their trees, such as its title, description and assignment text.
    - They can add cards to their trees, with both front and back texts.
    - They can edit the contents of existing cards.
    - They can remove cards from their tree.
- Tree owners can remove their trees.
- All users can view published trees on their respective tree view pages.
    - Users can click on cards whose parent has been revealed.
    - Cards will show their 'front'-text when they are clickable.
    - Cards will show their 'back'-text once they have been revealed by clicking on them.

## Localization

- All pages can be viewed and used in both Dutch and English, with Dutch being the default.
